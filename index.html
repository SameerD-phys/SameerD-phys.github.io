About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Physics at the University of Houston. My research focuses on understanding and controlling complex quantum systems using ideas from quantum information, many-body physics, and quantum optics.

I am particularly interested in developing quantum algorithms for open quantum systems and in using quantum optical probes to investigate interacting quantum materials.

Research

Open Quantum Systems

Quantum algorithms for simulating dissipative dynamics, thermalization, and non-equilibrium quantum systems.

Quantum Optics

Using entangled photons and cavity quantum electrodynamics to probe many-body quantum systems.

Quantum Materials

Exploring many-body phenomena and topological properties of quantum materials.
